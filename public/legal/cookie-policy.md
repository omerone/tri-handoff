## 1. Introduction

TRi uses cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your experience, improve security, and analyze how you use our Service. This Cookie Policy explains what cookies are, which cookies we use, and how to manage them.

**By using TRi, you consent to the use of cookies as described in this policy, unless you reject non-essential cookies.**

---

## 2. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device (computer, tablet, phone) that remember information about your visits. When you visit TRi again, your browser sends cookies back to our servers, allowing us to recognize you and remember your preferences.

**Similar Technologies**:
- **Local Storage**: Browser storage that persists longer than cookies
- **Web Beacons**: Invisible pixels used to track page views
- **Analytics Tags**: JavaScript code that tracks user behavior

This policy covers all these technologies under "cookies."

## 3. Cookie Categories

We use cookies in the following categories:

### 3.1 Essential Cookies (Always On)

**Purpose**: Provide core Service functionality. These are required for TRi to work.

| Cookie Name | Purpose | Duration | Consent Required |
|-------------|---------|----------|------------------|
| `session_id` | Maintains your login session | 30 days | No (essential) |
| `auth_token` | Authenticates your identity | 30 days | No (essential) |
| `tenant_id` | Identifies your organization/account | 30 days | No (essential) |
| `csrf_token` | Protects against cross-site forgery attacks | Session | No (essential) |
| `secure_session` | Ensures encrypted session | 30 days | No (essential) |

**Effect of Rejection**: If you disable essential cookies, TRi will not function. You cannot log in or use the Service.

**User Control**: Essential cookies cannot be disabled but are automatically deleted after 30 days of inactivity.

### 3.2 Analytics Cookies (Consent Required)

**Purpose**: Understand how you use TRi so we can improve features and performance. These are optional.

| Cookie Name | Provider | Purpose | Duration | Consent Required |
|------------|----------|---------|----------|------------------|
| `_ga` | Google Analytics | User identification for analytics | 2 years | Yes (opt-in) |
| `_ga_XXXXXXXXXX` | Google Analytics | Session tracking for analytics | 2 years | Yes (opt-in) |
| `_gid` | Google Analytics | Current session tracking | 24 hours | Yes (opt-in) |
| `_gat` | Google Analytics | Throttle request rate | 1 minute | Yes (opt-in) |

**What We Measure**:
- Pages visited
- Features accessed
- Time spent on each page
- Device type and browser
- Geographic location (city-level, anonymized)
- Click patterns and interactions

**Data Anonymization**: Google Analytics anonymizes IP addresses and does not identify individuals.

**User Control**: You can opt-out of analytics cookies via:
- Cookie Preferences during initial banner
- Settings → Privacy → Cookie Preferences
- Browser's "Do Not Track" signal (if enabled)
- Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on

### 3.3 Preference Cookies (Consent Required)

**Purpose**: Remember your preferences so you don't have to set them repeatedly.

| Cookie Name | Purpose | Duration | Consent Required |
|-------------|---------|----------|------------------|
| `theme_preference` | Dark/light/system mode | 1 year | Yes (optional) |
| `language_preference` | English/Hebrew language setting | 1 year | Yes (optional) |
| `consent_status` | Records your cookie consent choices | 1 year | Yes (optional) |
| `sidebar_state` | Remembers sidebar open/closed state | 1 year | Yes (optional) |

**User Control**: You can clear preference cookies anytime by:
- Deleting browser cookies
- Using incognito/private browsing mode
- Updating preferences in Settings → Preferences

## 4. Legal Basis for Cookies

**Under GDPR (EU/UK/EEA), cookies are governed by:**
- **ePrivacy Directive (2002/58/EC)**: Requires prior informed consent for non-essential cookies
- **GDPR (EU Regulation 2016/679)**: Requires lawful basis for processing
- **PECR (UK Privacy Regulations)**: Similar to ePrivacy Directive for UK users

**Our Cookie Consent Model**:

| Cookie Type | Legal Basis | Consent Required |
|------------|-----------|------------------|
| **Essential** | Contractual necessity (Art. 6(1)(b)) | No |
| **Analytics** | Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)) | Yes (opt-in) |
| **Preferences** | Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)) | Yes (opt-in) |

**Consent Mechanism**: 
- **First Visit**: Cookie banner displays explaining cookie types
- **User Choice**: "Accept All," "Reject All," or "Preferences" buttons
- **Preference Modal**: User can granularly control analytics and preference cookies
- **Stored Choice**: Your selection is remembered for 1 year via `consent_status` cookie

## 6. How to Manage Cookies

### 6.1 In-App Cookie Management

**Within TRi**:
1. Go to Settings → Privacy & Cookies → Cookie Preferences
2. Toggle analytics and preference cookies on/off
3. Click "Save Preferences"
4. Your choice is saved for 1 year

### 6.2 Browser-Based Cookie Management

**Most browsers allow cookie control:**

**Chrome**:
1. Settings → Privacy and Security → Cookies and other site data
2. View site data → Search "tri.com"
3. Delete individual cookies or block all

**Firefox**:
1. Options → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
2. Manage Exceptions → Search "tri.com"
3. Delete cookies or set to "Allow" or "Block"

**Safari (macOS/iOS)**:
1. Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data
2. Search "tri.com"
3. Remove or manage cookies

**Edge**:
1. Settings → Privacy, Search & Services
2. Clear Browsing Data → Cookies and Saved Website Data
3. Delete site data

### 6.3 Disable All Cookies

To disable all cookies:
1. Open browser settings
2. Navigate to privacy/cookies section
3. Select "Block all cookies"
4. Note: TRi will not function; you must rely on session-based access

### 6.4 Delete Cookies from TRi

**Manually Clear TRi Cookies**:
- Chrome: Settings → Privacy → Clear Browsing Data (select "Cookies")
- Firefox: Options → Privacy → Clear Recent History (select "Cookies")
- Safari: History → Clear History → All Time (includes cookies)

**Automatic Expiry**: All TRi cookies are automatically deleted after:
- Essential cookies: 30 days
- Analytics cookies: 2 years
- Preference cookies: 1 year

## 7. Third-Party Cookies

### 7.1 Third-Party Services

TRi integrates with third-party services that may set their own cookies:

| Service | Cookies Set | Purpose |
|---------|------------|---------|
| **Google Analytics** | `_ga`, `_gid`, etc. | Analytics and performance measurement |
| **CloudFlare** | `__cf_bm`, etc. | DDoS protection and WAF |
| **Stripe** | `s` (if payment page visited) | Payment processing |
| **MetaApi** | (if embedded) | MT5 account integration |

### 7.2 Third-Party Cookie Control

**Third-party cookies are set by external services, not directly by TRi.** To manage them:

- **Google Analytics**: Use Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on
- **CloudFlare**: Contact CloudFlare for options
- **Stripe**: Review Stripe's privacy policy
- **Browser Settings**: Set to block third-party cookies (may break integrations)

**Note**: We recommend allowing third-party cookies for TRi to function properly.

## 8. Cookie Consent Verification

### 8.1 Checking Consent Status

To verify your cookie preferences:

1. Open your browser's Developer Tools (F12 or right-click → Inspect)
2. Go to Application → Cookies → tri.com
3. Look for `consent_status` cookie
4. Value shows your consent choices (e.g., `essential=true&analytics=false&preferences=true`)

### 8.2 Consent Logs

TRi maintains logs of:
- When you granted/rejected consent
- Which cookies you accepted
- Your IP address and timestamp
- Browser and device information

These logs are used to ensure compliance with GDPR and ePrivacy regulations.

**Note**: Not all browsers support DNT; explicit cookie consent is the primary control.
