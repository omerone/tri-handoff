## 4. Data Retention

We retain personal data only as long as necessary for the purposes stated above. Specific retention periods:

| Data Category | Retention Period | Rationale |
|---------------|-----------------|-----------|
| User Account & Profile | Duration of account + 30 days after deletion | Service operation, legal holds |
| Trading Data | Duration of account + 7 years after deletion | Tax/regulatory requirements |
| MT5 Connection Token | Duration of connection + 30 days after disconnect | Service operation |
| Session Tokens | 30 days of inactivity (auto-expire) | Security |
| Password Reset Tokens | 1 hour | Security (prevent replay attacks) |
| Audit Logs (Security) | 12 months | Regulatory compliance, incident investigation |
| Failed Login Attempts | 90 days | Rate limiting, security |
| Email Communications | 2 years | Legal compliance, dispute resolution |
| Backup Data | 30 days (last 3 snapshots) | Disaster recovery |
| Deleted Account Data | 30 days (for recovery) | User recovery requests, legal holds |

After the retention period expires, data is deleted or anonymized and cannot be recovered.

**Data Deletion on Account Termination**  
When you delete your account, we immediately delete or securely archive:
- Account credentials
- Profile information
- Session tokens
- Personal preferences

Trading data is retained for 7 years to comply with tax and anti-money laundering regulations, unless you request deletion under "Right to Be Forgotten" (subject to legal exceptions).

## 7. Your Privacy Rights

### 7.1 GDPR Rights (for EU/EEA/UK residents)

You have the following rights under GDPR/UK GDPR:

#### Right to Access (Article 15)
**You may request a copy of all personal data we hold about you.**

- Submit a request at privacy@tri.com or through your account settings
- We will provide data in a portable, human-readable format
- Timeline: 30 days from receipt of request
- No fee (unless request is manifestly unfounded or excessive)

#### Right to Rectification (Article 16)
**You may correct inaccurate or incomplete personal data.**

- Update your profile directly in the application
- For other data, contact privacy@tri.com
- Timeline: 30 days

#### Right to Erasure / "Right to Be Forgotten" (Article 17)
**You may request deletion of your personal data.**

- Delete your account directly in Settings → Account → Delete Account
- We will delete all personal data within 30 days
- **Exception**: Trading data may be retained for 7 years for tax/regulatory compliance
- Backup copies are retained for 30 days for recovery purposes

#### Right to Restrict Processing (Article 18)
**You may limit how we use your data.**

- Contact privacy@tri.com to restrict processing
- We will suspend processing while honoring your request
- Timeline: 30 days

#### Right to Data Portability (Article 20)
**You may receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.**

- Request export via Settings → Privacy → Export Data
- Format: JSON or CSV
- Timeline: 30 days

#### Right to Object (Article 21)
**You may opt-out of specific processing activities.**

- For marketing: Unsubscribe from email newsletters via link in emails
- For analytics: Reject cookies via Cookie Settings
- For other processing: Contact privacy@tri.com

#### Right to Lodge a Complaint (Article 77)
**You may file a complaint with your local data protection authority:**

- **EU**: Contact your country's Data Protection Authority (search: [edpb.europa.eu](https://edpb.europa.eu))
- **UK**: Information Commissioner's Office (ICO): [ico.org.uk](https://ico.org.uk)
- **Israel**: Data Protection Authority: [justice.gov.il](https://www.justice.gov.il)

### 7.2 Non-EU Residents

If you live outside the EU/EEA/UK, your rights are governed by your local privacy laws. We will accommodate reasonable requests to exercise rights similar to GDPR rights.

## 8. Cookies and Tracking

### 8.1 What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device to remember preferences and track usage. We use cookies in three categories:

### 8.2 Cookie Categories

| Category | Purpose | Examples | User Control |
|----------|---------|----------|--------------|
| **Essential** | Session management, security, authentication | `session_id`, `auth_token` | Always on (required) |
| **Analytics** | Understand user behavior (optional) | Google Analytics (`_ga`, `_gid`) | Opt-in (reject by default) |
| **Preferences** | Remember your choices | `theme`, `language`, `consent_status` | Manage in settings |

### 8.3 Cookie Management

**When you first visit TRi**, you will see a cookie consent banner:
- Click **"Accept All"** to enable all cookies
- Click **"Reject All"** to enable only essential cookies
- Click **"Preferences"** to customize your choices

**To manage cookies after initial consent:**
- Settings → Privacy & Cookies → Cookie Preferences
- Or use your browser's cookie management tools

**Browser Controls**  
Most browsers allow you to:
- Block all cookies
- Allow only first-party cookies
- Delete cookies on exit
- View and delete cookies

Disabling non-essential cookies may reduce functionality (e.g., analytics) but will not affect core Service operation.

See Section 9 (Cookie Policy) for detailed information.
